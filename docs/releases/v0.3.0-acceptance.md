editing attempted to clone Vue proxies, the native shortcut did not reach the
frontend, and uninstall left the product registry key. Those defects were fixed
and the final candidate was rebuilt. Its automated smoke passed all six stages.

used synthetic task data, then uninstalled the app and restored the original
folders. The original `study.db` was restored at 196608 bytes with SHA-256
